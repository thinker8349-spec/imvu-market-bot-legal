IMVU Market AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how IMVU Market AI handles information when used in a Discord server. The bot provides support tickets, ticket transcripts, invite tracking, and related direct messages.

1. Information the Bot May Process

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and message or ticket identifiers needed to operate the bot.
  • Ticket information: messages sent in a ticket, ticket status, timestamps, and transcript content when a ticket is closed.
  • Invite information: invite codes or invite-related events and counts used to attribute and display invite totals.
  • Operational information: errors and event details needed to troubleshoot and maintain the bot.

The exact information processed depends on which bot features are enabled and how the server is configured.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To create, manage, and close support tickets.
  • To generate and retain ticket transcripts for customer support, moderation, and resolving disputes.
  • To calculate invite counts and send users direct messages about their invite activity.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the bot against abuse.

Ticket messages and invite information are not intended to be used to train AI or machine-learning models.

3. Storage and Access

Information may be stored outside Discord in the bot's hosting environment or configured database/files, including ticket transcripts and invite-tracking records. Authorized bot administrators or server staff who have the necessary permissions may access relevant records for support, moderation, and troubleshooting.

Do not use tickets to send passwords, authentication codes, full payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information.

4. Retention and Deletion

Ticket transcripts and invite records may be retained for as long as they are needed to provide the bot's features, maintain support records, investigate abuse, or resolve disputes. The bot administrator should delete records that are no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations and operational backups.

If you want to ask about a record associated with you, contact the bot administrator through the official IMVU Market Discord server. Deletion may not be immediate where records exist in backups or must be retained for legitimate operational reasons.

5. Direct Messages

The bot may send direct messages containing ticket-closure notifications or invite-count updates. Whether a message is delivered depends on your Discord privacy settings and whether Discord permits the bot to message you.

6. Sharing

The bot does not intend to sell personal information. Relevant ticket information may be visible to authorized server staff. Information may also be processed by Discord and the hosting or storage providers used to operate the bot, under their respective terms and privacy practices. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the bot, its users, or the server.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to restrict access to stored records and bot credentials. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should avoid placing sensitive credentials or payment information in tickets.

8. Your Choices

You can adjust Discord privacy settings that control direct messages from server members or bots. You may also contact the bot administrator with questions about information processed by the bot. Some information is necessary for ticket handling and invite tracking to function.

9. Children's Privacy

The bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children outside the rules and age requirements that apply to Discord. Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements in their region.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the bot's features, storage, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the bot administrator through the official IMVU Market Discord server or the support contact provided there.
